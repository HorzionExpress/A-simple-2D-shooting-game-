Hey there! 👋 Welcome to my very first game project — I'm super excited to share this with you!

‌Precision Shooter‌ is a casual browser-based shooting trainer built entirely with vanilla JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas. No heavy game engines, no fancy frameworks — just pure, messy, hand-crafted code from someone who decided to jump into game dev headfirst. 🎯

What's this all about?
Ever wanted to warm up your aim without launching a 50GB FPS? This is it. Click on moving targets, rack up points, unlock new guns, and watch your stats climb. Simple concept, surprisingly addictive.

How to play
‌
Left click‌ to shoot, ‌R‌ to reload
‌
1–0‌ to switch weapons (once unlocked)
‌
F1/F2/F3‌ to swap between your backpack loadout
‌
Right-click / E‌ to scope in with the AWM sniper
‌
ESC / P‌ to pause and tweak settings

Earn score to unlock better weapons — or just cheat, no judgment here 😏

What's in the box
‌
10 weapons‌ — from a humble Glock to an RPG-7. Each has its own recoil, spread, fire rate, and reload time
‌
6 environments‌ — city, desert, forest, snow, trench warfare, and a classic shooting range
‌
Backpack system‌ — pick 3 favorites and quick-swap mid-game
‌
4 ammo types‌ — standard, armor-piercing, high-explosive, and tracer rounds
‌
Stats tracking‌ — score, kills, accuracy, headshots, empty clips, streaks — the whole nine yards
‌
Customizable crosshair‌ — change color, size, and shape
‌
Weather effects‌ — rain and snow because... why not? 🌧️❄️
‌
Local save‌ — your progress sticks around between sessions
‌
Cheat mode‌ — infinite ammo, full auto, unlock everything. For when you just wanna blow stuff up 💥

This is my first crack at making a game, so the code is probably held together by hope and duct tape. If you find bugs, have ideas, or just wanna say hi, feel free to open an issue or a 
PR! Happy shooting! 🎉
